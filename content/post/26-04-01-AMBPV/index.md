We publish a review paper in Adv. Mater. on the theory and experimental advances in bulk photovoltaic effect and its application.

<!--more-->

The bulk photovoltaic effect belongs on one promising nonlinear optics process that converts light field into a static electric current or voltage. Our group has been devoted into this field for a few years, focusing on its emergence in various quantum materials such as ferroelectrics, topological insulators, (anti-)magnets, and helimagnets. Invited by the Advanced Materials, we wrote a Review article that describe the basic concept, mechanism, experimental evidences, and future challenges on the bulk photovoltaic effect. The work has been online recently, and one may refer to https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.202517783 for more details.
